=== FILE frontend/src/pages/library/LibraryDeskPage.tsx
>>> SUMMARY
**The librarian's desk**:
1. **Issue a book**: type the student's USN, search the catalogue, and press "Issue" next to a book. Each book shows a small coloured cover (its colour from the title), its ISBN, and "2 / 3" copies on the shelf with a bar (amber when one copy is left, red when none). The backend checks that a copy is available, the student's 3-book limit, and (with the book's version) that another librarian did not take the last copy at the same moment.
2. **Books on loan**: every open loan, most overdue first, with the student's avatar, the due date (a red "Overdue" badge when late), the days late, the fine so far, and a "Return" button. Returning asks for confirmation and shows the fine to collect.
