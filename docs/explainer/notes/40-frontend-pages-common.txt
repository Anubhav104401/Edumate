=== FILE frontend/src/pages/LoginPage.tsx
>>> SUMMARY
**The sign-in screen** at `/login`. The person types a username and password and presses "Sign in". What happens next:

1. The browser checks that both boxes are filled ("Enter both your username and your password.").
2. `login()` sends `POST /api/auth/login` with `{"username":"student","password":"Edumate@2026"}`.
3. The backend checks the bcrypt hash, the lockout counter and the account, and answers with a **token** and the user's details, or with an error:
   - 401 "Invalid username or password." (the message never says **which** of the two was wrong, so attackers cannot find out which usernames exist);
   - 423 "Too many failed attempts. Your account is locked until 10:45 AM." (15 minutes after the fifth failure)
4. On success, the token is saved and the person is sent to the page they originally asked for, or to the dashboard.

**The demo helper.** In development builds only, a list of demo accounts appears under the form. Clicking one fills in its username and the demo password. A production build (`npm run build`) removes the helper, because `SHOW_DEMO_ACCOUNTS` is false there.

**Every text** on this page is in `messages.ts` under `login`: the title, labels, button, hint and account descriptions.

>>> 22

>>> 24-25

>>> 59-64

=== FILE frontend/src/pages/DashboardPage.tsx
>>> SUMMARY
**The home page** after login: "Welcome, Aarav Sharma", then a grid of coloured **cards**. The backend decides which cards the person's role sees (`DashboardService`), and the frontend just draws them. Each card is a link to the page where its number can be acted on.
>>> 15-16
The drawing starts. `<>` groups the parts without an extra box.
>>> 17
The title "Welcome, <name>" and a subtitle.
>>> 18
While waiting: "Loading…".
>>> 19
If loading failed: the red box with a "Try again" button that calls `reload`.
>>> 20
If there are no cards at all: a grey message.
>>> 21-23
Otherwise, a grid with one tile per card.
>>> 24
Each tile is a link (`<Link>` changes the page without reloading). Its class, e.g. `stat tone-warn`, gives it its colour. The label, big value and hint are drawn inside it.
