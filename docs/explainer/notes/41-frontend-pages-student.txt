=== FILE frontend/src/pages/student/MyFeesPage.tsx
>>> SUMMARY
**"My fees"**, for a student (or "Your child's fees", for a guardian). It shows:
1. a card with the **amount outstanding** (amber if something is due, green if nothing is);
2. the **fee demands** (bills), each with its amount, paid, due, due date, a status badge (Paid / Due / Overdue) and a **"Pay now"** button;
3. the **payments** made, with the order number, status and receipt number;
4. the **ledger** (account statement): every debit (a bill) and credit (a payment).

→ the browser moves to the (simulated) bank page /pay/ORD-81D4440CAE9D45BA</pre>

=== FILE frontend/src/pages/student/MockGatewayPage.tsx
>>> SUMMARY
**The pretend bank page** at `/pay/<orderId>`. In real life this page belongs to the payment gateway (Razorpay, PayU…), and the student would enter card or UPI details there, **never on EduMate**. Here it is simulated, because a real gateway needs a merchant account.

It shows the order and amount, and two buttons, **Approve payment** and **Decline payment**. The interesting part is the **"Send the bank’s confirmation"** choice ("once (normal)", "2 times (replay test)", "3 times (replay test)"): 1, 2 or 3 times. Real gateways sometimes send the same "payment succeeded" message more than once, which caused defect DEF-014 in the report (the student was credited twice). Choosing 2 or 3 lets you **watch the fix working**: the first delivery says PROCESSED with a receipt, and the others say DUPLICATE_IGNORED. The ledger is credited once.

**Behind the buttons:** `POST /api/payments/gateway/mock/<orderId>/complete {"success": true, "deliveries": 2}`. The backend's `MockPaymentGateway` then builds the callback exactly as a real gateway would, **HMAC-signs** it with the shared secret, and delivers it to the real callback endpoint the chosen number of times.
>>> 26-33
`complete(success)`: busy; asks the simulated gateway to finish the payment (approve or decline) with the chosen number of deliveries; shows the outcomes and reloads the order; shows any error; and is no longer busy.

=== FILE frontend/src/pages/student/HallTicketPage.tsx
>>> SUMMARY
**The hall ticket page**: is the student allowed to sit the coming exams, course by course, and **why**?

At the top, a green "You are eligible in every course. Your hall ticket will be issued." or an amber "You are not yet eligible in every course. The reasons are listed below." box, the fee status, and when exams start. Then a table with, for each course: attendance, whether internal assessment is complete, whether a medical exemption applies, the decision (eligible or not), the **decision-table rule** that decided it (R1–R5), and the reasons.

Showing the rule and reasons, rather than only "not eligible", means a student knows exactly what to fix, and staff can check every decision against the regulation (the report's decision-table test cases TC-008 to TC-010).

>>> 22

>>> 39-43

>>> 45
