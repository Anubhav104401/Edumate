=== FILE frontend/src/pages/student/MockGatewayPage.tsx
>>> SUMMARY
**The pretend bank page** at `/pay/<orderId>`. In real life this page belongs to the payment gateway (Razorpay, PayU…), and the student would enter card or UPI details there, **never on EduMate**. Here it is simulated, because a real gateway needs a merchant account.

It shows a **checkout card**: a bank icon, a "Secured with a signed (HMAC) callback" chip, the amount in large numbers, the order number and status, and two buttons, **Approve payment** and **Decline payment**. The interesting part is the **"Send the bank’s confirmation"** choice ("once (normal)", "2 times (replay test)", "3 times (replay test)"). Real gateways sometimes send the same "payment succeeded" message more than once, which caused defect DEF-014 in the report (the student was credited twice). Choosing 2 or 3 lets you **watch the fix working**: the first delivery says PROCESSED with a receipt, and the others say DUPLICATE_IGNORED. The ledger is credited once.

**After approving**, a green circle **draws itself** and a tick draws inside it (SVG `pathLength` animation), **confetti** bursts from both lower corners, and the list of deliveries slides in one line at a time. After declining, a red cross appears instead, and no confetti.

**Behind the buttons:** `POST /api/payments/gateway/mock/<orderId>/complete {"success": true, "deliveries": 2}`. The backend's `MockPaymentGateway` then builds the callback exactly as a real gateway would, **HMAC-signs** it with the shared secret, and delivers it to the real callback endpoint the chosen number of times.

**Confetti and the security policy.** The confetti library normally draws in a background **Web Worker** created from a `blob:` address. EduMate's Content-Security-Policy (in `nginx.conf`) does not allow workers from `blob:`, so the confetti is drawn on the page's own `<canvas>` with `useWorker: false`. It also honours "reduce motion": no confetti at all for those visitors (`disableForReducedMotion`).

=== FILE frontend/src/pages/student/MyTimetablePage.tsx
>>> SUMMARY
**"My timetable"**: the published week. For a student or guardian it shows the student's section; for a teacher, their own lectures, with the section shown in each cell (a teacher teaches several sections). Data: `GET /api/timetable/me`.

Two cards:
1. **"Today"**: today's lectures in order, as a row of coloured cards (period and time, course, room, and the teacher, or for a teacher the section). The lecture running right now glows. On a day without lectures: "No lectures today. Enjoy the break."
2. **"The whole week"**: the whole week as a grid (`TimetableGrid`), with today's row highlighted.

Each loan is a **card with a little book cover**: a coloured rectangle with the title's first letter, its colour worked out from the title (`hueFor`), so every book has its own, always the same, colour.
